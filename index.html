        Rules at a glance

        Race your four pegs from your pen, clockwise around the track, into your home row. First to get all four home wins.

        CardMovePlay againLeave penLeave bull's eyeSpecial

        Rules for every turn

        Your turnHold five cards, play one, make its move. A card without "play again" ends your turn and you draw back to five. Drag your cards into any order you like, even while others are playing.
        When you're stuckIf no peg can go forward, one must go backward by the card's value. With no pegs on the track, the card does nothing.
        BumpingLand on another color and that peg goes back to its pen.
        Your own pegsNever land on or jump over your own color, forward or backward.
        HomeExact count to enter and to move inside. Pegs at home are safe. A peg that backed up past its home entrance can go straight in.
        Fast trackLand exactly on a colored inner corner and that peg is on the fast track. It may hop corner to corner to your own color, or carry on around the main track. Until it moves off, you must move it first: no other peg, no leaving the pen. Blocked by your own color, it goes back to the pen.
        Bull's eyePass a corner with exactly one step left to drop into the center. Only a King, Queen or Jack brings it out, onto your own corner.
        Splitting a 7Both parts go forward and add up to exactly 7. The first part can't stop on a fast-track corner.
